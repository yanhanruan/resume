Here's a comparison of my resume from 9 months ago with the one that recently got me an offer at Google.

I can only hypothesize, but I think my success was aided by several key differences. I expanded my original multi-line bullet points into punchier, and more factual, single-line bullet points. I tried to let my points speak for themselves, with respect to what they say about me. I modified the content to reflect my capacity for working with large code-bases. I switched to a serif font, and italicized and emboldened certain segments, e.g., I italicized all job titles. I removed the skills section, and added my industry relevant courses. I added my GPA. There are likely more differences, but these are the ones that I could easily discern.

Even with my updated resume, I still struggled to get interviews. But, I believe it helped significantly in securing my interview with Google.
